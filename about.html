About Us

Billiards Bahamas is the premier destination for professional pool tournament coverage in The Bahamas. We specialize in live streaming high-quality billiards content, bringing the excitement of competitive pool to audiences worldwide.

Our Mission

To showcase the incredible talent of pool players in the Bahamas and throughout the The Bahamas region, while providing fans with unparalleled access to live tournament action and professional matches.

What We Do

We provide comprehensive coverage of pool tournaments and matches, including:

  • Live tournament streaming with professional commentary
  • High-definition video production
  • Multi-camera coverage of championship matches
  • Post-tournament analysis and highlights
  • Player interviews and behind-the-scenes content

Our Team

Our dedicated team of videographers, producers, and billiards enthusiasts work tirelessly to bring you the best pool content from the Bahamas. With years of experience in both sports production and the game of pool, we understand what fans want to see.

Community

We're more than just a video production company—we're a community of pool lovers dedicated to growing the sport in The Bahamas. Through our broadcasts, we aim to inspire the next generation of players and bring recognition to the incredible talent that exists in our region.
