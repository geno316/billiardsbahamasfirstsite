Tournaments

We cover the most prestigious pool tournaments throughout the Bahamas and the Caribbean region. Our comprehensive tournament coverage includes everything from local championships to international invitational events.

Featured Tournaments

Bahamas National Pool Championship

The premier annual event showcasing the best pool talent in the Bahamas. This multi-day tournament features 8-ball, 9-ball, and 10-ball divisions with competitors from across all islands.

Caribbean Pool Masters

An invitational tournament bringing together top players from throughout the Caribbean. This prestigious event features significant prize money and attracts international attention.

Nassau Open

A popular open tournament held quarterly in Nassau, welcoming both amateur and professional players. Known for its competitive atmosphere and exciting finals.

Islands Challenge Series

A traveling tournament series that visits different islands throughout the year, promoting pool talent across the entire Bahamas archipelago.
