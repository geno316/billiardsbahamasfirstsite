Quarterly Events

Several major tournaments occur on a quarterly basis:

  • Nassau Open - Held every three months
  • Islands Challenge Series - Rotating monthly between islands
  • Regional qualifiers for national championships

Annual Championships

Major annual events to watch for:

  • Bahamas National Pool Championship - Summer
  • Caribbean Pool Masters - Winter
  • Year-End Championship Tournament

Special Events

Throughout the year, we also cover special exhibition matches, charity tournaments, and invitational events featuring international players. These are announced on our social media channels as they're scheduled.

How to Stay Updated

Never miss an event by:

  • Subscribing to our YouTube channel with notifications enabled
  • Following our social media accounts
  • Checking the community tab on YouTube for announcements
  • Visiting this page regularly for schedule updates

Time Zones

All events are scheduled in Eastern Standard Time (EST/EDT). International viewers should adjust accordingly. Livestreams are saved for replay viewing at any time.

Venue Information

Our events are held at various pool halls and tournament venues across the Bahamas. Specific venue information is provided when each event is announced.
